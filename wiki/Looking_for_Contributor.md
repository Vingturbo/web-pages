# Looking for Contributors

## Introduction

We are actively developing an open-source project on GitHub, hosted at:  
https://github.com/Vingturbo/web-pages.git  
Our team is currently small, and we are sincerely inviting developers who share our vision to join us and help move the project forward.

---

## Project Overview

This project is a pure HTML-based web initiative, aimed at building a **fast, convenient, and highly compatible** web experience. Our goal is to ensure it runs smoothly across a wide range of devices—including **phones, computers, and tablets**—and to support as many operating systems with network access as possible.

HTML has a relatively low learning curve. If you are willing to invest time in learning, we believe you will gain valuable skills and grow alongside the project.

---

## What We Expect from Contributors

To ensure efficient and orderly project progress, we hope you possess the following basic qualities:

### 1. Basic Git Proficiency
- You should be comfortable with common Git commands (e.g., `init`, `pull`, `commit`) to keep collaboration smooth.
- If your Git experience is limited, we can provide learning materials (such as the *ProGit* eBook) to help you get up to speed.

### 2. Objective Observation and Problem-Sensing Ability
- We value contributors who can critically examine the project from a user or tester's perspective, identifying issues and areas for improvement.

### 3. Creativity and Design Thinking
- The project is still in its early stages, and we greatly need fresh ideas and constructive feedback to drive iteration and evolution.

### 4. Sense of Discipline and Rule-Following
- We have established basic standards for code submission and quality. We expect you to understand and follow them to maintain consistency and maintainability.

### 5. Clear Commit Messages and Change Logs
- For every code submission, please clearly describe **what** was changed and **why**. This is crucial for future traceability, debugging, and version management.
- We assume everyone—including ourselves—can make mistakes. Fixing them is part of the learning and growth process for both you and the project.

### 6. Inclusiveness and Team Spirit
- Our members come from diverse backgrounds. We expect mutual respect, understanding, and a positive, healthy collaborative atmosphere.

### 7. Compliance with Platform Rules and Laws
- Advertising, spamming, distributing malicious content, or any illegal activities are strictly prohibited within the team. Violators will be immediately removed and reported.
- If you cannot agree to this requirement, please do not apply to join.

> **Special Note:**  
> If you are unsure whether you meet all the above criteria, you are still welcome to reach out. We will conduct a friendly preliminary conversation or test. Even if you lack experience, as long as you are motivated and willing to learn, we will evaluate your case seriously.
